Acme Analytics — 2024 Company Overview
======================================

About Acme Analytics
--------------------
Acme Analytics is a B2B software company founded in 2018 and headquartered in Austin, Texas.
The company builds tools that help mid-market businesses turn operational data into
actionable insights. Acme's mission is to make trustworthy analytics accessible without
requiring a dedicated data science team.

Leadership
----------
- CEO: Maya Chen
- CTO: Omar Patel
- Head of Customer Success: Priya Nair

Products
--------
1. Acme Pulse
   A real-time dashboard product for monitoring KPIs across sales, marketing, and support.
   Pulse supports live connectors to Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zendesk.

2. Acme Forecast
   A demand-forecasting product that uses historical order data to predict inventory needs
   for the next 4–12 weeks. Forecast is designed for retail and e-commerce operations teams.

3. Acme Guard
   A data-quality monitoring product that alerts teams when pipelines break, schemas drift,
   or metric definitions change unexpectedly.

Customers and Markets
---------------------
Acme focuses on mid-market companies with 200–2,000 employees in North America.
Top industries served: retail, SaaS, logistics, and financial services.
In 2024, retail customers contributed 41% of ARR, SaaS contributed 27%, logistics 18%,
and financial services 14%.

Support and SLAs
----------------
Acme offers Standard and Enterprise support tiers.
Standard support responds within 1 business day.
Enterprise support responds within 4 business hours and includes a dedicated success manager.
Uptime SLA for Enterprise customers is 99.9%.
